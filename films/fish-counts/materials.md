## Data

From the site's repo, github.com/kenny-yukich/oregon-fish-forecast-concept at commit 20d0bdd (2026-11-04), `data/counts.js` (Columbia River DART, refreshed daily by the site's workflow):

- Bonneville Dam, through Nov 3: 4,496 adult Chinook, coho and steelhead in 7 days (1,200 + 3,193 + 103). Year to date vs same date last year: Chinook 583,029 / 632,035 (92%), coho 97,002 / 159,781 (61%), steelhead 63,292 / 155,947 (41%).
- The Dalles Dam, through Oct 31: 3,691 (1,399 + 1,570 + 722). Chinook 261,528 / 360,178 (73%), coho 35,841 / 55,536 (65%), steelhead 48,468 / 129,708 (37%).
- Willamette Falls, through Oct 31: 442 (6 + 347 + 89). Chinook 38,763 / 33,096 (117%), coho 8,577 / 21,806 (39%), steelhead 17,660 / 9,232 (191%).
- Adults only (jacks and the wild-steelhead subset left out), as on the site. Counts show run timing, not catch rates.
